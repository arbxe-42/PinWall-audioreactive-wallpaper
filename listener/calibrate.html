Speaker delay

Measures how late you hear sound on the speakers you're using right now (Bluetooth speakers or headphones, the laptop speakers, headphones), so the wallpaper's hits land exactly when you hear the beat.

1. Make sure the speakers you want to measure are the Windows output and the Pin Wall listener is running.
2. Press start. You'll hear 4 count-in clicks, then 24 clicks.
3. Tap the space bar (or click the box) exactly on every click you hear, from the fifth click on.

The result is saved to listener/devices.json for the current output device.
