While playing: K tap a kick, S tap a snare (yellow). X marks the detector hit nearest the playhead as a misfire. Z undo.

With the mouse (in the big view): click an orange / cyan label to mark it wrong (click again to undo). Click a white (old) / pink (KickNet, live now) hit to mark it a misfire. Click an empty spot in the yellow lane to add a kick there (shift-click: snare); click a yellow mark to delete it.

Tap delay: taps land late: Bluetooth speakers (the K3s) add ~250 ms and reacting to a sound adds up to ~300 ms more. The page starts from the speaker delay the listener uses and, with "learn it from my taps" on, measures your real delay from your first taps (the median gap to the drum hit just before each tap). Tap a few clear kicks first. Everything saves on its own to bench/annotations/<track>.json.
